IMAGE PROMPT — JOURNEY VIDEO OPENING FRAME (anonymous factory bay)
Purpose: first frame of the journey video. CONFIDENTIALITY IS THE POINT: the
factory must be impossible to place — no signage, no text, no flags, no
recognisable machinery branding, nothing written anywhere in the scene.

Photorealistic wide interior of a dark, clean industrial fabrication bay at
night. A single factory-new 40ft high-cube shipping container in freshly
painted RAL 7035 light grey stands on low steel stands in a pool of cool
overhead light, surrounded by darkness. Robotic gantry welding arms are poised
along its side with a few faint drifting sparks. Polished concrete floor with
subtle reflections. Camera at low three-quarter angle near the container's
front corner, ready to glide along its length.

Absolutely no text, characters, signage, posters, safety notices, flags,
logos or brand markings anywhere in frame. No people. No rust, dents or
weathering — the container is showroom-new. Photorealistic, cinematic
lighting, 16:9, minimum 1920x1080.
