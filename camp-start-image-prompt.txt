IMAGE PROMPT — CAMP VIDEO OPENING FRAME (the "before" state)
Purpose: this image is the FIRST FRAME of the camp-build video. It must feel
like the same site as the finished camp aerial (camp.jpg), just before the
build — so keep the same red earth, the same distant ranges and mine
infrastructure on the horizon, and a light state that can naturally progress
to dusk by the end of the video.

Photorealistic low drone view, about 2 metres above the ground, three-quarter
angle on a single factory-new container accommodation unit in freshly painted
RAL 7035 light grey, standing alone on a vast freshly graded red-earth pad at
an Australian mining site. Late golden afternoon light, long soft shadows.
Around the unit, the empty pad stretches out with faint grader lines and
survey pegs with string lines marking out future building rows — hinting at
the village to come. On the horizon: low rocky ranges, scattered spinifex
scrub, and distant mine infrastructure with a few small lights, matching the
finished camp aerial. Deep blue sky warming to orange at the horizon.

Clean, new, engineered — no rust, no dents, no weathering. No people, no
vehicles, no animals, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Photorealistic, 16:9,
minimum 1920x1080.
